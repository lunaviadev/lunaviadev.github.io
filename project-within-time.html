The Premise

"Six months remain before the fate of two worlds is sealed and humanity as it's known ceases to exist."

As the CEO of Team INNERMINDS, Within Time is our flagship commercial project. The story follows Yunis Caelum and his childhood friends—Estella Kisiel, Angie Alfaro, and Rictor Fitzsimmons—as they venture into the mysterious world of Iolade. Players piece together a dark prophecy to put an end to the Scion of Time's terror and save both Iolade and Terrus.

Our goal is to replicate the nostalgic feel of the classic SNES JRPG era, elevated with sleek modernization, deep customisation, and a massive narrative scale.

The "STANCE" Combat System

Combat in Within Time throws you into deeply tactical encounters using our proprietary STANCE System. Picking up to four active characters per fight, players navigate tough encounters where every move counts.

Exploiting enemy weaknesses, landing critical hits, dodging, and blocking generate 'momentum'. High momentum unlocks devastating mechanics like 'DUAL SKILLS' and 'STANCE SKILLS'. However, misplays cause momentum to plummet, empowering the enemy. With heavy Elemental Synergy integrated across all 13 characters, success requires planning three moves ahead.
