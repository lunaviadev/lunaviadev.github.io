Within Time

Overview

Within Time is a TBRPG that I began developing back in 2023 in an effort to create my first commercial game. It evolved into a large-scale project I’ve dedicated most of my time to, built with the Godot engine and developed alongside my indie team “Team INNERMINDS”.

Development Process

The project began in 2023 with early prototypes in Unity, before transitioning to Godot for greater flexibility and cleaner scripting through GDScript. As the lead developer, I handled all game systems including dialogue, combat logic, and UI/UX implementation, while coordinating with artists and composers under Team INNERMINDS.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Advanced GDScript development and modular architecture
  • Team collaboration using Git version control
  • Pixel art and UI design integration
  • Project management and iterative design
