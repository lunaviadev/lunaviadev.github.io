Within Time

Project Overview

Within Time is a TBRPG that I began developing back in 2023 in an effort to create my first commercial game. It evolved into a large-scale project I’ve dedicated most of my time to, built with the Godot engine and developed alongside my indie team “Team INNERMINDS”.

The game follows the story of Yunis Caelum and his friends as they delve into a world previously unknown to mankind. Within Time aims to replicate the feel of the classic JRPG era of the SNES whilst adding a level of modernization and uniqueness to truly stand out.

The "STANCE" Combat System

Within Time features a unique take on turn-based combat, utilizing our proprietary 'STANCE' system that rewards tactical play and deep knowledge. Exploiting enemy weaknesses, gaining critical hits, dodging, or blocking all contribute to your 'momentum'.

Higher momentum allows you to utilize powerful mechanics like 'DUAL SKILLS' or 'STANCE SKILLS'. However, misplays cause your momentum to drop quickly, allowing the enemy to overpower your team. Success requires planning three moves ahead.

Development History

Originally starting as 'Our Mind' in 2022, the project began as a prototype in RPGMakerMV. While a demo was released to Steam, I wasn't satisfied with the engine's limitations. This led to a long restructuring period where we explored Unity and Game Maker Studio 2 before finally settling on Godot in early 2025.

Over the last 10 months, we’ve completely rewritten the story, revamped the art style, and officially formed Team INNERMINDS. We are currently preparing to release a high-fidelity demo by the end of the year.
