Within Time

Overview

Within Time is a TBRPG developed with the Godot engine and Team INNERMINDS. It's a love letter to the SNES JRPG era, modernized with deep tactical systems and high-fidelity pixel art. The story follows Yunis Caelum and his companions as they navigate a dangerous world in search of their lost friend, June Clavis.

Gameplay & "Stance" System

The core of Within Time is the "STANCE" system. Players must manage momentum by exploiting weaknesses and executing perfect blocks. Higher momentum unlocks Dual Skills and Stance Skills, rewarding players for planning several moves ahead.
