Colourways

3D Puzzle Platformer

UCA Technical Art Game Jam Minimalist Core Paint Mechanics

Project Overview

Developed alongside Harry-Dillamore, BradleyCurtis, and princessbleach, Colourways is a 3D puzzle platformer that challenges players with intuitive core paint mechanics. Created as a standout submission for the UCA Technical Art Game Jam, the game relies heavily on momentum, rapid puzzle-solving, and precise mechanical execution.

With an overwhelmingly positive reception regarding its engaging difficulty curve and concept, we are currently planning to expand its core systems into more complex levels and interactable elements.
