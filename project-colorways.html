Colourways

A minimalist 3D puzzle platformer revolving around intuitive core paint mechanics.

Project Overview

Developed alongside Harry Dillamore, Bradley Curtis, and Zoe Efstathiou, Colourways is a 3D puzzle platformer that challenges players to master rapid movement through environmental painting. Created as a standout submission for the UCA Technical Art Game Jam, the game relies heavily on momentum, rapid puzzle-solving, and precise mechanical execution.

Future Development Plans

Following an overwhelmingly positive reception after our showcase at Guildford Games Festival 26 regarding its engaging difficulty curve and accessible concept, the team is currently looking to expand the core systems into a fully fleshed-out title. We plan to introduce more complex level geometry, interactive elements, and entirely new ways to utilize the paint mechanics in 3D space.
