Protocol: Shutdown

Overview

Protocol: Shutdown was a short cyberpunk-themed action project created during a 2024 game jam. The goal was to explore atmospheric level design, tight controls, and a story that could be told entirely through visuals and sound without text or dialogue. Despite the time constraint, the result became one of my most polished small-scale projects.

Gameplay

Players infiltrate a hostile corporate complex, using stealth and quick reflexes to disable security drones and reach the system’s core. The focus was on creating responsive movement and using environmental lighting cues to guide the player. Visual effects and shaders were used to heighten tension and immersion.

Development Process

Developed in just 7 days using Godot 4, the project emphasized speed and modular design. My primary focus was refining the control system and camera smoothing to maintain immersion while keeping performance optimal. I also handled sound design and environmental storytelling through visual details and lighting direction.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Godot 4 scene organization and scripting
  • Lighting, color design, and shader-based effects
  • Optimized 2D camera and physics
  • Sound and ambient design
