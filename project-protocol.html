Protocol: Shutdown

A high-octane twin-stick cyberpunk shooter.

The Jam Challenge

Created in just 7 days for Brackey’s Gamejam 2025.2, Protocol: Shutdown tasks players with saving retro computer systems from a consciousness-consuming virus. Developed in tight collaboration with Bradley Curtis and Keisha Byrne, it perfectly encapsulates rapid, high-pressure prototyping and delivery.

Unique Mechanics

We implemented an aggressive "Discard Ability" system—you can only hold one weapon at a time, but discarding a gun triggers a powerful special ability. This forces players to constantly cycle through their arsenal in chaotic firefights. Drawing heavy inspiration from dungeon crawlers, we developed a fluid movement system complete with dodge rolling and highly responsive controls to counter varied enemy AI.
