Protocol: Shutdown

The Jam Challenge

Created in just 7 days for Brackey’s Gamejam 2025.2, Protocol: Shutdown is a high-octane twin-stick shooter. Developed in collaboration with Bradley Curtis and Keisha Byrne, the game tasks players with saving retro computer systems from a consciousness-consuming virus.

Unique Mechanics

We implemented a "Discard Ability" system—you can only hold one weapon at a time, but discarding a gun triggers a powerful special ability, forcing players to constantly cycle through their arsenal. The game also takes heavy inspiration from dungeon crawlers in order to feel unique and immersive. Theres a fluid movement system with dodge rolling and responsive controls amongst differing enemies with varying attacks to continiously spice up exploration.
