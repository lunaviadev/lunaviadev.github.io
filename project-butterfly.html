Like a Butterfly

Narrative Design

Like a Butterfly is an experimental visual novel built in Unity. It explores a "day in the life" of a transgender girl, focusing on the subtle emotional nuances and daily hurdles she faces. This project serves as a study in atmospheric storytelling and pacing. This is a work in progress project that is currently on halt but holds deep importance to me as it aims to present struggles I have personally been through in a digestable but uncomfortable format as you place yourself in the shoes of someone like myself in order to feel powerful story telling and the emotional weight that comes with something like being transgender in the modern day.

Technical Details

  • Custom Unity Narrative Framework
  • Dynamic UI Scene Flow
  • Emotional Lighting Systems
  • Original Music Integration
