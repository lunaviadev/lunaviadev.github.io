hey! i'm josie (lunavia online) — a 20-year-old trans game developer from the UK. welcome to my little corner of the internet!

i'm a full-time Games Development student heading into my 3rd year, and honestly i spend most of my time buried in game engines, pixel art, and probably too many cups of tea.

right now i'm actively developing two games:

> Within Time — my flagship TBRPG built in Godot. i'm the CEO & Lead Dev of Team INNERMINDS, overseeing everything from systems architecture to narrative & art direction.
> BCKSPCE — a psychological thriller visual novel told entirely through a retro desktop interface. already live on Steam!

outside of game dev i make music (lots of guitar, some FL Studio), draw pixel art & digital paintings, and sink way too many hours into FFXIV. big fan of anything with that early-2000s internet energy — webcore, Y2K aesthetics, the whole thing.

feel free to poke around. enjoy your stay ▌
