Background

Self-taught since the age of 14, when early experimentation with RPG Maker and JavaScript laid the groundwork for a serious interest in game development. What followed was years of self-directed learning across engines and languages, developing fluency in C# through Unity and GameMaker Language through Game Maker Studio, building that knowledge through shipped projects rather than formal instruction.

Unreal Engine has been part of the toolkit for three years, most notably as the foundation for Colourways. In 2024, Godot became the engine of choice for all primary development. More recently, the skillset has expanded into Steamworks P2P multiplayer networking through OVRLOAD. That same self-directed sensibility extends to visual work: eight years as a pixel artist, serving as lead artist on Within Time, have made art direction and asset production as native to the process as writing code.
