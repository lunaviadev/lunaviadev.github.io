Background

Self-taught since the age of 14, when early experimentation with RPG Maker and JavaScript laid the groundwork for a serious interest in game development. What followed was years of self-directed learning across engines and languages, developing fluency in C# through Unity and GameMaker Language through Game Maker Studio, building that knowledge through shipped projects rather than formal instruction.

Unreal Engine has been part of the toolkit for three years, most notably as the foundation for Colourways. In 2024, Godot became the engine of choice for all primary development. Within Time, BCKSPCE, and One Final Winter are each built on its architecture, a deliberate consolidation around a platform that rewards disciplined systems thinking. That same self-directed sensibility extends to visual work: eight years as a pixel artist, serving as lead artist on Within Time, have made art direction and asset production as native to the process as writing code.

About

A bit about me

I'm Josie, a trans game developer from the UK in my final year of a BSc in Games Development, graduating 2027.

As Founder and Lead Developer of Team INNERMINDS, I oversee all areas of Within Time, my flagship TBRPG currently in development on Steam. I'm also the solo developer of both BCKSPCE (live on Steam) and its sequel One Final Winter, as well as having worked on an unreleased multiplayer title.

Outside of game dev I make music, draw pixel art and digital paintings, and spend too much time in FFXIV. Big fan of early-2000s internet energy and all things webcore.
