About Me

Hi! I'm Liliana Bellas, also known as Lunavia online. I’m a 20-year-old UK-based developer and full-time Game Development student.

I'm currently in the process of making my first commercial game 'Within Time' alongside my studio 'Team INNERMINDS'.

I'm familiar with many engines and languages, specialising mostly in GDScript/Godot and C#/Unity. I also have knowledge in Unreal Engine, C++, HTML/JS, and small amounts of C.

I create all my artwork with Clip Studio Paint (traditional digital art) or Aseprite (2D pixel art) and use an XP Pen 15.6 Pro as my art tablet.

For music I use FL Studio 21 and play electric guitar, which I often use for my recorded tracks.

I’ve been programming for roughly 3 years and drawing for over 5. If any of my work interests you, you can contact me on Discord — ‘lunavia.gd’.
