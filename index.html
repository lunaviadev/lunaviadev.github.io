Programming Projects

Within Time

Main project in development.

Within Time is a TBRPG that I began developing back in 2023 in an effort to create my first commercial game. It then later evolved into being a largescale project, in which I decided to dedicate a large majority of my time to. Within Time is created with the Godot game engine and has been using said engine since Janurary of 2025. The game is directed by me and involves collabrative efforts from my indie team 'Team INNERMINDS', a small company I started alongside the creation of Within Time in order to outsource help so that I would not be creating this game alone. Team INNERMINDS has grown to a size of 8 and also supports a large amount of voice actors working on the project.

Protocol: Shutdown

Made for Brackey's Gamejam 2025.2

Protocol: Shutdown was a gamejam gam made in under a week as part of Brackey's Gamejam 2025.2. It was made in collaboration with a fellow developer Bradley Curtis and I created and provided all assets seen within the game alongside contributing to parts of the programming. Protocol:Shutdown was made in Unity.

Like a Butterfly

On Hiatus

Like a Butterfly is a visual novel developed in Godot that tells the story of a transgender girl and her daily lives. It was originally created as a social commentary on how difficult it can be to be transgender and was a passion project of mine, though has been temporarily placed on hiatus in order to allow me to focus on other projects.
