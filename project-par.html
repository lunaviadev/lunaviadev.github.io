Par for the Course

The Concept

Currently in the early prototyping phase, Par for the Course is a technical exploration into physics-based arcade gameplay. While the core theme centers around golf, the mechanics focus on momentum, environment destruction, and precision controls.

Development Goals

  • Custom Physics Engine Integration
  • Procedural Level Generation
  • Advanced Particle & Collision Systems
  • Dynamic Camera Panning
